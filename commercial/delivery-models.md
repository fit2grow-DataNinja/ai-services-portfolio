# Delivery models

Status: Agreed commercial direction

## Shared proposition

One defined business problem, reusable methodology, agreed acceptance criteria and business deliverables. Choose technology and operating model based on the client.

## A — Client-operated (preferred when suitable)

Build/configure internal shared AI skill or dedicated application; train staff; hand over operating instructions, tests and ownership. Charge fixed setup fee plus optional recurring maintenance and advisory retainer.

## B — Provider-managed

Provider operates workflow, checks output and delivers recurring business result. Charge setup fee plus recurring service fee. Clearly scope manual review, delivery frequency, source access and responsibilities.

## C — Client-operated with managed maintenance

Client executes day-to-day; provider maintains methodology, templates, connectors, tests, supports staff and recommends improvements within an agreed allowance. This is an especially relevant recurring-revenue model.

## Meeting approach

Present A and B as alternative ways to obtain the same outcome, including ownership, responsibilities, support burden, deployment and total cost. Do not assume skills are automatically cheaper or easier to share: permissions, licensing and connections vary.
