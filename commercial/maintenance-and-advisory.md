# Maintenance and advisory agreement principles

Status: Agreed direction; commercial terms not set

Provider serves as external methodology and technical knowledge specialist and a contact for issues, employee questions, relevant trends and improvement opportunities. Client retains business-rule approval, data ownership and decision accountability.

## Potential recurring scope

Methodology and KPI review; template updates; integration compatibility checks; regression tests; bounded incident/question support; onboarding help; periodic improvement review; advice on relevant AI developments.

## Contract boundaries to define per client

Included hours, response times, review cadence, supported versions, change-request process, exclusions, responsibility for credentials and infrastructure, data handling, escalation, offboarding, knowledge transfer and ownership of deliverables.

Avoid unlimited support and undefined promises to monitor all AI developments. Major enhancements are separately scoped.

## Economics

Track implementation hours, customization hours, monthly support hours, platform/API costs, expected incidents and net revenue per support hour. Pricing and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.
