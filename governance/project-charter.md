# Project charter

Status: Approved scope | 2026-10-21

## Objective

Build a portfolio of generic but client-customizable B2B AI-enabled services, initially feasible for a solo operator, that address well-defined and preferably recurring business problems.

## Founder capabilities

Business analysis across multiple domains; digital B2B/B2C analytics, measurement and audits, A/B testing, dashboards and reports, awareness and lead generation, SEA knowledge, hands-on SQL and databases, experience with highly integrated systems. Data science experience is more limited; prefer verified deterministic calculation libraries and manageable implementation complexity.

## Target market

EU SMEs and mid-market companies first; selective departmental opportunities at larger companies. Prefer EU hosting and EU-operable technologies, but no blanket requirement for EU-only vendors.

## Technology scope

Low-code plus Python, SQL, APIs, scripts, containerized deployment. Shared AI skills and structured AI workflows are both in scope.

## Commercial principles

- Business outcome and client pain point take precedence over architecture.
- Prefer client-operated solutions with ongoing maintenance/advisory; also offer provider-managed solutions.
- Do not build a standalone application if an internal shared skill satisfies the requirements equally well.
- Use defined deliverables, bounded support, documented methodology, and explicit acceptance criteria.
- Assess recurring support burden and client-specific customization effort.

## Exclusions for now

High-difficulty candidates from active implementation; unsupported claims of GDPR compliance; unverified pricing; multi-tenant SaaS as a default; unnecessary infrastructure components.
