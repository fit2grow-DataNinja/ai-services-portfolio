# Research methodology

Status: Agreed approach, with provisional scoring

## Sequence

1. Identify recurring business pains and target users.
2. Compare internal shared skill, internally integrated solution, and external offering.
3. Verify relevant vendor/technology capability from current official docs; cross-check independent sources when possible.
4. Assess fit with architecture v0.1 and flag any proposed changes.
5. Assess technical difficulty, customization, UI usability, and EU suitability.
6. Assess client-operated and provider-managed delivery economics.
7. Validate strongest candidates with representative data, tests and customer discovery.
8. Only then promote to a recommended portfolio.

## Difficulty (1 easy to 5 difficult; proposed weights)

Core implementation 25%; data/integrations 20%; client customization 20%; testing/reliability 15%; UI/deployment 10%; security/governance 10%. Weighted 1.00–2.00 Low, 2.01–3.20 Medium, 3.21–5.00 High/defer. These are uncalibrated thresholds; high-risk security/consequential decisions may override score.

## Commercial attractiveness (proposed weights)

Pain severity/frequency 25%; cross-client repeatability 25%; demonstration/UI clarity 20%; measurable value 15%; delivery/revenue potential 15%.

## Additional gates

Internal substitution risk (High/Medium/Low); setup and customization hours; support hours per month; client self-service; recurring revenue per expected support hour; data availability; procurement/security requirements; whether the skill-only option is sufficient.

## Evidence quality

For each factual claim: publisher, URL, source date/version (if stated), checked date, claim, limitations, confidence. Separate vendor functionality from our design proposals and untested feasibility. Do not turn earlier brainstorming scores into validated ratings.
