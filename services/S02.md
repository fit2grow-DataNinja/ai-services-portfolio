## Evidence and boundaries
The shared synthetic case reports **202 contacts, 121 deals, 92 medium missing-owner findings**. Variant 1's 11-test suite was user-reported successful; Variant 2 has a committed 17-test suite and the owner confirmed successful fresh-checkout execution. Variant 2 issue-level parity was reported; raw checkout logs and multi-user Skill distribution/version consistency remain unverified.

Both variants are **synthetic-data only**. SEC-001 is not implemented; missing-owner policy is provisional; full freshness/close-date reconciliation and missing-association inference from partial exports remain out of scope. GitHub CI and commercial hardening are deferred. No customer-data or production-readiness claim.
