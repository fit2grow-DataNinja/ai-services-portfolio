# Business areas and initial opportunities

Status: Research backlog; not validated market demand

## Priority 1 — Marketing and sales performance

Campaign performance reviews, lead funnel diagnostics, tracking/measurement audits, landing-page audits. Familiar analytical territory; check whether source data and KPI definitions are stable enough for a repeatable offer.

## Priority 2 — Data reliability and reporting

Data freshness/quality checks, KPI and dashboard QA, recurring performance reporting, anomaly investigation. Prefer deterministic checks and auditable issue registers.

## Priority 3 — Business knowledge and documentation

SQL/business-rule documentation, data dictionaries, requirements/process documentation, knowledge assistants. Simple generators face high internal-skill substitution; differentiate through verified source handling, validation and maintenance.

## Specialist offering

A/B testing methodology and analysis; strong existing asset, but may not be a recurring need for low-traffic small businesses.

## Research order

Evaluate customer pain and delivery economics before expanding technology. Do not assume the ranking above is evidence of market demand.
