# Customer segments

Status: Hypotheses requiring interviews

## A. Small businesses

Limited technical capacity; recurring reporting, marketing and operational pain. Offer low-friction, fixed-scope, affordable solutions with minimal setup. Could prefer managed delivery or an easy shared skill; do not assume preference.

## B. SMEs with analysts or specialists

Have business knowledge but limited engineering capacity/time. Offer internal skill implementation, validation, integrations, training, and recurring support. Initial priority alongside segment A.

## C. Larger companies

Target narrow specialist or departmental engagements rather than enterprise-wide platform replacement. Procurement, security, references and delivery capacity may be significant barriers.

## Discovery questions

Who owns the problem? How often does it occur? What is the current workaround? What tools and skills already exist? What data can be accessed? What is the cost of errors? Who pays? What are support and security requirements?
